# AI Engineer Role Note :
## What does an AI engineer do?
Focuses on integrating AI models into applications and delivering working AI-powered solutions
The role is a blend of software engineering and machine learning/AI technologies
The AI Engineers will be working with existing models, LLMs, APIs, RAG, automation etc  to build useful system 
## AI engineer VS ML Engineer VS Data Scientist:
### ML engineer
Focuses on developing, training and deploying machine learning models and pipelines
### Data Scientist:
Focuses on analyzing data, identifying patterns and building predictive models prototype to support business decisions
### When should we not Use AI?
Not prefered when the problem can be solved with simple rules
AI may also be unsuitable when the cost, complexity,  or consequences of incorrect predictions out weight its benefits

## Business Cases
 ### Suitables:
 ### Case 1: Customer Support Chatbot

**Problem:** A business gets many repetitive customer questions and it is interrupting the system 

AI Solution: implementing an LLM-powered chatbot with RAG that answers questions based on company provided documentation which saves time on repetitive tasks and enables quicker information retrieval 

### Case 2: Document Data Extraction

**Problem**: A company spends a lot of time entering information from forms manually

AI Solution: Build a system that extracts important information from documents automatically which saves time by reducing manual data entry using OCR to extract text from documents and LLM or NLP to identify important information such as dates, numbers,totals

### Case 3 : risk detection :

**Problem**: working place that need to track risk managing protective equipment managing (Safety Helmet, vest..etc)

AI Solution: Build an AI assistant that checks through live cameras on working people using models like yolov8 which reduces risks on people live

### unsuitable Case: employee attendance tracking

**Problem**:  A company needs to track employee attendance and calculate working hours

**Why AI Is Not Needed**: This task can be handled using a regular software system with employee check-in/check-out records and predefined rules
Using AI would add unnecessary complexity and cost without providing such big benefits
